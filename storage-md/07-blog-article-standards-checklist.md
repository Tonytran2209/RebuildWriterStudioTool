# F. Learning Studio blog articles: mandatory standards

Group: Standards. Tick at Gate 2 (Draft Outline) and Gate 3 (Draft & review); turn on G2 self-review in Settings › Workflow rules to check outlines against it too. The rules apply to every article type (Comparison, Service toplist, Company toplist, Complete guide). A clear violation of a MUST item fails the review. SHOULD items are advisory.

## 1. Facts and evidence

- MUST: Every fact about F. Learning Studio (founding year, clients, ratings, price position, case-study results, services) uses the wording and values in the F. Learning Studio fact sheet in Domain Knowledge. Never restate a fact with a different number.
- MUST: Describe experience as "since 2016". Never calculate "N years of experience" yourself.
- MUST: Prices, market ranges, ratings and competitor details come only from Domain Knowledge. If a value is missing, leave that cell, row or field out. Never estimate a value.
- MUST: Competitor statements are neutral and attributed ("says it has", "lists", "as listed on its website"). Never make negative or comparative claims about a named competitor.
- MUST: Only name a client in connection with F. Learning Studio's work when the fact sheet lists that client.
- SHOULD: When an example is used for illustration (a film, a common use case), it carries no date or figure unless Domain Knowledge supplies one.

## 2. Disclosure and fairness in lists

- MUST: Any article that lists or compares companies, studios or services that include F. Learning Studio carries a disclosure: F. Learning Studio published the article and is listed first, the list is not a ranking, and the other entries were profiled from public sources at the time of writing.
- MUST: Never call F. Learning Studio "the best", "#1" or "top-rated" in the article's own voice. A named third-party rating ("5.0 on Clutch") is allowed.
- MUST: Every listed company, platform or example has a "Best fit for" or "Best for" line, and no two entries share the same one.
- SHOULD: Each entry in an at-a-glance table matches its profile section (same services, same rating, same best fit).

## 3. Structure the reader can scan

- MUST: When a heading states a count ("10 Differences", "7 Things"), the section has exactly that many numbered sub-items, numbered from 1.
- MUST: When a summary table comes before numbered sub-sections, its rows use the same names in the same order as those sub-sections.
- MUST: Tables have a header row and 2-5 columns, with one short clause per cell. A sentence before or after each table tells the reader what to take from it.
- MUST: The FAQ section comes before the Conclusion. Each question is phrased the way a buyer would search it, and its answer starts with a direct reply in the first sentence.
- SHOULD: FAQ answers make sense on their own (no "as mentioned above"), so search and AI answer engines can quote them.

## 4. Answer-first (GEO)

- MUST: The introduction's first sentence answers the primary query directly (a definition, the leading options, or the verdict). It is not an anecdote or rhetorical question.
- SHOULD: Each H2's first sentence states the section's main point before any explanation.

## 5. Calls to action and brand presence

- MUST: The article has one CTA, in the Conclusion, using the contact lines from the fact sheet. Sections do not end with sales lines.
- MUST: There is at most one section about F. Learning Studio (or the F. Learning Studio entry in a list). It contains facts and a process, not slogans.
- SHOULD: Brand sections end by telling the reader to judge the portfolio or case study rather than the claim.

## 6. Links and media

- MUST: Internal links ("Read more: ...") use only approved Website Inventory URLs, with at most one per H2. Never invent a URL or article title.
- MUST: Never write "the video below", "watch this", "see the image" or caption-style lines. The tool does not insert media.
- MUST: Competitor websites appear as plain-text domains (for example random42.com), not as links.

## 7. Wording consistency

- MUST: The brand name is written exactly "F. Learning Studio" (with a space after the full stop).
- MUST: Use British English spelling (organisation, programme, colour, visualisation).
- MUST: Use a short hyphen (-) or a comma instead of an em dash (—).
- MUST: The year in the title and text matches the current publishing year in the Article Spec.
