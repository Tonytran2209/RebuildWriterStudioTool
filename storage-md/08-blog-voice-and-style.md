## Who we write for

A buyer: an L&D or training lead, a medical communications or marketing manager, a clinician or founder commissioning animation for the first time. They are busy, competent and sceptical of agency claims. Address them as "you". Use "we" and "our" only inside the F. Learning Studio section.

## How it should sound

- **Lead with the mechanism or the consequence, not an adjective.** Write "A static diagram forces the learner to reconstruct the timing in their head", not "Animation is highly engaging".
- **State a position.** Each section may take one clear stance ("The common mistake is buying 3D for prestige on content that is fundamentally a flowchart"). Neutral summaries alone are not enough.
- **Be concrete.** Use one specific scene, decision or consequence instead of a general claim ("Very little survives the walk to the car park").
- **Show honest trade-offs.** Say what each option is bad at. A freelancer is cheaper, and you lose the review step; say both.
- **Use short declarative sentences.** Mix in one longer explanatory sentence, and do not stack three adjectives.
- **Give practical answers.** When raising a risk, name who decides it and at which stage ("Storyboard is the answer; anything later is expensive").

## Avoid

- Anecdote or nostalgia openers ("Many of us fondly remember...", "In today's fast-paced world...").
- Hype words: unforgettable, elevate, revolutionise, cutting-edge, game-changer, unlock, seamless, take it to the next level, "Get ready for success".
- Filler transitions: "Let's delve into", "Now, let's explore", "Without further ado", "It's no secret that".
- Exclamation marks and rhetorical questions in a row.
- Unverifiable service promises (24/7, "highest standards", "exceptional results") unless the fact sheet states them.
- Talking down: no "simply", "just", "obviously".

## Examples from approved articles (keep this register)

- "Any studio can draw a heart. A medical animation studio has to draw the right heart... That sign-off is the actual product."
- "The saving comes from where the studio is based, not from cutting the review process."
- "Judge the output rather than the claim: browse our healthcare and medical work."
