## Medical animation companies

### Random42 Scientific Communication
- Website: random42.com
- Based in: London, England, UK. Founded in 1992.
- Specialties: medical and scientific animation, interactive design, scientific websites, VR, AR, MR.
- Claim: says it has worked with all of the top 25 pharma companies.
- Best fit for: large pharma launches and immersive or interactive formats.

### 3FX
- Website: 3fx.com
- Based in: Blue Bell, Pennsylvania, US.
- Specialties: mechanism of action (MOA) and mechanism of disease (MOD) animation, medical illustration, congress and event visuals, medical AR and VR, interactive experiences.
- Claim: says it has more than 28 years in the industry.
- Best fit for: MOA and MOD animation for pharma and biotech.

### Cast Pharma
- Website: cast-pharma.com
- Based in: New York, NY, US.
- Specialties: medical animation and explainer videos, illustrations and infographics, scientific slide decks.
- Named clients: Boehringer Ingelheim, Johnson and Johnson, AstraZeneca, Roche.
- Best fit for: pharma teams that need animation plus supporting materials.

### AXS Studio
- Website: axs3d.com
- Based in: Toronto, Ontario, Canada.
- Specialties: medical device animation, scientific animation, interactive experiences, illustrations.
- Named clients: AstraZeneca, Bayer Healthcare, Pfizer, Nature.
- Best fit for: medical device mechanisms and launches.

### Cognition Studio
- Website: cognitionstudio.com
- Based in: Seattle, Washington, US.
- Specialties: science communication, brand and digital strategy, design strategy.
- Recognition: lists AMI Awards of Excellence, Muse Advertising Awards and a 2023 Cooper Hewitt National Design Award nomination.
- Best fit for: research organisations that need animation inside a wider brand or campaign.

### XVIVO Scientific Animation
- Website: xvivo.com
- Based in: Wethersfield, Connecticut, US. More than two decades of experience. Research, scriptwriting, animation, editing and delivery are done in-house.
- Specialties: 3D and medical animation, medical illustration, pharma marketing, science communication.
- Named clients: Pfizer, National Institutes of Health, Harvard University, Roche.
- Best fit for: cellular and molecular storytelling for pharma and research.

### 3D for Science (3DforScience)
- Website: 3dforscience.com
- Based in: Spain.
- Specialties: medical animation, scientific illustration, interactive applications, medical VR and AR.
- Named clients: Ogilvy Health, Thermo Fisher Scientific, Roche.
- Best fit for: interactive or VR training rather than a linear video.

### Nucleus Medical Media
- Website: nucleusmedicalmedia.com
- Based in: Kennesaw, Georgia, US.
- Specialties: medical device animation, pharma and biotech animation, medical animation for education, licensed content from an existing library.
- Named clients: Nature, Boston Scientific, WHO.
- Best fit for: custom work, or licensing existing animation when the budget is tight.

### Ghost Productions
- Website: ghostproductions.com
- Based in: United States.
- Specialties: medical animation, surgical animation, medical devices, VR surgery and surgical simulation.
- Named clients: 4C Medical, AstraZeneca, Boston Children's Hospital.
- Best fit for: surgical procedures and surgical training.
