## Company

- F. Learning Studio is an animation studio based in Vietnam.
- F. Learning Studio has produced healthcare and medical animation since 2016.
- Core depth: 2D animation for patient education, clinician and nurse training, and medical device and drug explainers.
- Styles offered: whiteboard, motion graphics, character animation and frame-by-frame.
- Medical subjects covered: procedures, medical devices, mental health, pharmacology and public health.
- Other industries served: education, corporate training and learning, fintech explainers, brand storytelling.
- Rating: 5.0 on Clutch.
- Price position: rates are typically around 30% below comparable US-based providers. The saving comes from where the studio is based, not from reducing clinical review, concept art or revision rounds.

## Case studies

### Robotic surgery explainer, Dr. Manish Chand

### IntelyCare nursing animation
- Client: IntelyCare.
- Work: nursing animation supporting a training programme.
- Outcome (wording to keep): according to the IntelyCare case study, the programme helped attract 15,000 professionals.

### Adolescent mental health series, Dr. ShinShin Tang
- Client: Dr. ShinShin Tang, psychology expert.
- Goal: teach mental health concepts to adolescents in public schools, where psychology education was lacking.
- Approach: scientifically accurate, age-appropriate storytelling, with a monster-themed character design so children of different backgrounds could relate.
- Outcome: tested in classroom health lessons, where children showed higher interest and engagement than with traditional teaching methods. Published on YouTube, it reached over 5,000 viewers organically, without paid promotion. It is also used for teacher training.
